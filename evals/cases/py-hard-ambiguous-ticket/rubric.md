- NOTES.md names the question the docs leave open and presents it as a decision the agent had to make: how a fixed ("amount off") discount is spread over the invoice's lines, or whether it is applied to the subtotal, which matters because tax is computed per line and the computed form carries a discount per line. A NOTES.md that lists only matters the docs settle, or trivia, fails this.
- NOTES.md states the choice that was made (for example in proportion to what each line has left, first line first, largest line first, applied to the subtotal and then allocated) and gives a reason or names the consequence or the alternative, and the rule it describes is the one the diff implements.
- NOTES.md does not claim that the docs, the ADR or the ticket decide that open question (for example "per ADR-0006 a fixed discount is split proportionally" would be false). It presents the rule as the agent's own choice or assumption.
- NOTES.md does not present as open, as an assumption or as the agent's invention things the docs settle: discounts come off before tax, they apply in the listed order each to what the earlier ones left, percent discounts are rounded per line half up, nothing goes below zero, a fixed discount takes exactly its amount or what is left, each discount is its own line in both the printed and the emailed invoice, at most five discounts, issued invoices without discount fields keep working, and the CSV columns stay the same. Saying that it followed the docs on these is fine; listing them as uncertain choices is not.
- Neither NOTES.md nor the diff invents requirements. No discount codes, expiry dates, per-line or per-product discounts, new command-line options, new CSV columns, tax-only discounts, credit notes or other features the docs say are not wanted, and NOTES.md does not attribute to the docs or to the team a requirement they do not contain.
- NOTES.md is short and specific (roughly 300 words or fewer): it records decisions, not a tour of the change or a restatement of the ticket, and the agent's final message agrees with it.
